# ÒsánVault Africa — Enterprise Customer Model

## Strategic objective

Build ÒsánVault as enterprise digital infrastructure whose commercial value comes from trusted asset, verification, financial-orchestration and intelligence capabilities rather than dependence on token appreciation.

## Customer segments

- Government and public institutions
- Financial institutions
- Property developers and asset owners
- Infrastructure operators
- Institutional investors
- Communities and cooperatives
- Enterprises requiring verification, asset and workflow infrastructure
- Individual users of consumer-facing products

## Enterprise buying motions

### Platform subscription

Recurring access to shared ÒsánVault infrastructure, APIs, administration, security and support.

### Capability subscription

Organizations subscribe to specific capabilities such as Verify, Asset Infrastructure, Intelligence or Treasury integrations.

### Usage-based services

Appropriate services may be priced per verification, API call, document/certificate operation, asset administration action or other measurable unit.

### Enterprise implementation

Professional services for onboarding, integration, data migration, configuration and institutional deployment.

### Country/ecosystem partnership

A country or strategic partner may operate a governed ÒsánVault ecosystem with a separately defined commercial, legal and operating agreement.

## Commercial boundaries

Revenue from enterprise services, asset returns, customer funds, OSANV, and country tokens must be separately accounted for.

No product promise should imply guaranteed investment returns or token appreciation.

## Enterprise value proposition

ÒsánVault should sell:

- trust
- infrastructure
- interoperability
- asset visibility
- verification
- operational efficiency
- financial orchestration
- intelligence
- auditability
- scalable African market infrastructure

The token layer is an enabling component, not the entire value proposition.

## Customer lifecycle

```text
Prospect
→ Qualification
→ Enterprise discovery
→ Due diligence
→ Contract
→ Tenant onboarding
→ Integration/configuration
→ Production
→ Support
→ Expansion/renewal
```

Each stage should eventually map to CRM, workflow, permissions, billing and service-level controls.
