# ÒsánVault Africa — Enterprise Operating Model

## 1. Purpose

ÒsánVault Africa is being designed as an enterprise digital infrastructure company, not as a token-only project and not merely as a collection of applications.

The operating model separates the platform foundation, commercial modules, asset ecosystems, country ecosystems, and corporate governance so the company can scale without repeatedly rebuilding its core.

## 3. Customer segments

Primary enterprise segments:

- Governments and public institutions
- Financial institutions
- Property developers and asset owners
- Infrastructure operators
- Institutional investors
- Communities and cooperatives
- Enterprises requiring asset/verification infrastructure
- Individual users through consumer-facing products

Commercial packaging must distinguish enterprise contracts from consumer features.

## 4. Revenue architecture

Revenue must not depend on OSANV appreciation.

Potential revenue streams include:

- Enterprise subscriptions
- Platform/API licensing
- Asset registration and administration
- Verification and certification services
- Institutional integration fees
- Investment infrastructure/service fees where legally permissible
- Treasury/integration services where legally permissible
- Tokenization/structuring services where legally permissible
- Intelligence and analytics subscriptions
- Country ecosystem/platform participation fees
- Professional implementation and enterprise support

Each revenue stream requires separate legal, operational and accounting treatment.

## 5. Product ownership

Every commercial module must have:

- product owner
- technical owner
- business owner
- security owner
- compliance owner where applicable
- service-level definition
- revenue/cost model
- lifecycle status

A module may be independently deployed and commercially packaged without becoming an independent platform.

## 6. Financial separation

The following are separate concepts:

```text
Company Revenue
≠ Asset Value
≠ Customer Funds
≠ Treasury Ledger
≠ OSANV Balance
≠ Country Token Balance
```

The internal ledger is the accounting authority for platform-controlled financial records. Token balances are token-system state and do not replace corporate accounting.

## 7. Country expansion

Country onboarding is registry- and governance-driven.

A country activation must define:

- legal entity/partner structure
- regulatory analysis
- market owner
- country data requirements
- supported asset classes
- currency/payment rails
- verification sources
- country token policy, if any
- treasury/settlement model
- customer support and SLA
- data residency/privacy requirements

No country token is automatically activated by adding a country record.

## 8. Enterprise governance

High-impact operations require explicit authority boundaries:

- asset verification approval
- ownership changes
- investment product approval
- financial settlement
- treasury controls
- token issuance/contract changes
- country activation
- privileged access
- AI-assisted actions affecting material records

All material actions must be auditable.

## 9. AI governance

AI is an operational capability, not an autonomous corporate authority.

AI may analyze, summarize, classify, recommend and automate low-risk workflows subject to policy. Material financial, ownership, compliance, security or token actions require explicit authorization and auditable controls.

## 10. Enterprise service levels

Service classes should be defined before commercial commitments are made:

- Core platform
- Enterprise production
- Institutional critical
- Internal development

SLAs should cover availability, support response, incident handling, maintenance, data recovery and security obligations.

## 11. KPIs

The enterprise KPI framework should include:

### Commercial
- recurring revenue
- contract value
- customer acquisition
- retention
- module adoption
- enterprise expansion

### Platform
- active organizations
- verified assets
- API usage
- verification turnaround
- platform availability
- incident rate

### Financial operations
- settlement success
- reconciliation accuracy
- exception rate
- transaction processing time

### Trust and security
- verification accuracy
- security incidents
- privileged actions
- audit coverage
- recovery test success

## 12. Strategic rule

ÒsánVault should remain valuable as an enterprise infrastructure business independently of speculative token activity. OSANV and future country tokens are components of the ecosystem, not the sole source of enterprise value.
